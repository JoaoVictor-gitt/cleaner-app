# Images

Place your own photos in this folder using exactly these file names.
While a file is missing, the site shows a discreet placeholder instead.

| File                  | Used in                     | Suggested subject                                              | Recommended size            |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| `hero.jpg`            | Home — hero section         | Modern, spotless living room, natural light, neutral tones     | 1600 × 1200 px or larger (landscape) |
| `service-regular.jpg` | Services — Regular Cleaning | Cleaner tidying a modern home                                  | 1200 × 825 px (16:11)       |
| `service-deep.jpg`    | Services — Deep Cleaning    | Detailed cleaning of a kitchen or bathroom                     | 1200 × 825 px (16:11)       |
| `service-tenancy.jpg` | Services — End of Tenancy   | Empty, modern, perfectly clean property                        | 1200 × 825 px (16:11)       |
| `service-office.jpg`  | Services — Office Cleaning  | Modern, organised, clean office                                | 1200 × 825 px (16:11)       |

Framing:
- Photos fill their frame with `object-fit: cover`, centred. The hero frame is 6:5 and the service frames are 16:11,
  so a landscape hero photo loses a little on the left and right. Keep the main subject in the centre.
- Images are never stretched or distorted.

Tips:
- Use only photos you own or are licensed to use (e.g. your own shoot, or a stock licence that covers commercial use).
- Export as JPG, ideally under 400 KB each.
- To use another format (e.g. `.webp`), update the paths in `index.html` (hero) and `js/data.js` (services).
